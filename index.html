JPG and PNG

JPG uses lossy compression, which makes files smaller but can reduce image quality. PNG uses lossless compression and can support transparent areas.

Why convert JPG to PNG?

PNG can be useful for images that contain text, screenshots, diagrams, logos, or graphics where keeping sharp details is important.

Keep in mind

Converting a JPG to PNG does not restore quality that was already lost from JPG compression. The PNG keeps the image as it currently looks.

JPG or PNG?

Use JPG for

  • Photographs
  • Smaller file sizes
  • Sharing images

Use PNG for

  • Graphics and logos
  • Screenshots
  • Images that need transparency

About transparency

Converting a JPG to PNG does not automatically remove its background. The PNG format can store transparent areas, but removing a background requires an image editing tool.
